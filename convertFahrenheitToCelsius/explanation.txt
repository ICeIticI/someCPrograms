The reason they differ clicks into precedence. When
introducing parenthesis, that has among the highest
precedence over multiplication, division, addition,
and subtraction. The non-parenthesis version is wrong
because 100 gets divided by 180, which get multiplied
by the passed fahrenheit value.. and then it finally
gets subtracted by 32, whereas 32 was supposed to be
already subtracted from the passed fahrenheit value
before it got multiplied.
